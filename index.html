    01Abstract

    Holding on to who is in the video gets harder the more the video has to move. Chronos treats identity preservation as a temporal problem and places an anchor on every action segment.

    Identity-preserving video generation aims to synthesize high-quality and visually realistic videos from textual and visual conditions while faithfully maintaining the subject's identity across diverse motions. Prior methods have extensively introduced reference-guided control signals and identity-aware conditioning to improve facial alignment from reference images. However, improving static identity alignment alone creates a fundamental tension between identity fidelity and temporal controllability, which frequently causes identity drift or motion suppression under large pose variations and multi-segment sequential actions.

    To address this, we propose Chronos, a framework that unifies single-segment facial identity preservation and multi-segment sequential action generation under a common temporal formulation. Specifically, Chronos integrates Semantic Prompt Anchoring to adaptively refine the user prompt, Visual Frame Anchoring to transform conditions into visually grounded keyframe anchors for visual guidance, and Metric-Guided Candidate Routing to select the highest-quality videos from heterogeneous generators via multi-dimensional evaluation. Extensive evaluations demonstrate the superiority of our approach, which achieved tied first place in Track 1 and first place in Track 2 of the ACM MM 2026 Identity-Preserving Video Generation Challenge, demonstrating its effectiveness across both facial identity preservation and sequential action generation settings.

    02Method

    One temporal formulation for both tracks.

    Given reference images 𝓘, a raw prompt P and optional temporal captions 𝓒 = {ct}t=1T, Chronos generates a video that keeps the target identity while following the scene, the actions and their order.

    T = 1Track 1 · facial identity preservation, a single segment
    T > 1Track 2 · sequential actions, one anchor per segment

    1. Stage 1 · SPA

      Semantic Prompt Anchoring

      A non-intrusive prompt refinement that strengthens identity constraints while keeping the original scene, style and motion.

      • Identity cue extraction. A GPT model reads the reference images and keeps only stable, invariant identity cues H, dropping transient attributes such as clothing and background.
      • Risk-aware prompt adaptation. It estimates the identity-drift risk of the prompt as a score s ∈ {1, 2, 3} and adds concise viewpoint and visibility cues to produce P̂.
      1 Light · minimal correction 2 Medium · identity-aware adjustment 3 High · strong identity protection

    2. Stage 2 · VFA

      Visual Frame Anchoring

      Text alone is ambiguous when the target pose, action or viewpoint departs from the reference. VFA uses an image-to-image operator ΦI2I to turn 𝓘, P̂ and an instruction template into visually grounded keyframes.

      • Track 1. A single high-fidelity first-frame anchor A1 initializes image-to-video generation.
      • Track 2. Anchor A1 comes from the first caption c1; each later anchor At is generated from ct and the previous keyframes, in a cascade.
      A1A2⋯AT

    3. Stage 3 · MGCR

      Metric-Guided Candidate Routing

      Generators G1…GK each turn (P̂, 𝓐) into a candidate video. An automatic evaluator scores every candidate on text consistency, identity, subject preservation, motion, visual quality and an LLM-based holistic judgment.

      R(Vk) = Σj λj sj(Vk)

      V* = arg maxVk ∈ 𝓥 R(Vk)

      • Exception-aware review. If the winner falls below a threshold τj on any critical metric, it goes to human review instead of being accepted on its total score alone.

    Chronos is training-free: no model is fine-tuned. The generator pool uses Wan2.2 I2V-14B for both tracks, plus Seedance for Track 1 and Wan FLF2V for Track 2; SPA and VFA use GPT-based models.

    Ablation study

    • SPA strengthens identity preservation, raising the Subject Score on Track 1.
    • VFA improves visual quality across generator backbones (Seedance and Wan variants), most visibly in Aesthetic and Natural Score.
    • Full Chronos, with metric-guided routing, gets the best Total Score on both tracks.
